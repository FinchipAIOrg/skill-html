Current capabilities

These capabilities come only from the current repository SKILL.md, scripts, assets, and references, with a strict separation between structural support, deterministic checks, and decisions reserved for qualified professionals.

01

Report-type routing

Keeps CARE 2013, CONSORT 2025, SPIRIT 2025, ICH E3/E3 Q&A, and aggregate-safety routes distinct.

02

15 fail-closed templates

The generator copies fixed JSON/CSV assets only—no interpolation, clinical completion, or default overwrite.

03

Deterministic structure checks

Checks keys, statuses, and source-fact references for CARE, ICH E3, CONSORT 2025, and SPIRIT 2025.

04

Provenance & consistency

Checks local locators, SHA-256 metadata, claim links, dates, units, n/N percentages, and totals without choosing an authoritative source.

05

Aggregate AE formatting

Accepts aggregate rows plus sidecar metadata; rejects patient/subject/case identifiers, narratives, and onset dates.

06

Bounded auxiliary checks

Terminology checks schema/syntax or exact local-dictionary matches; de-identification checks process documentation. Radiology, pathology, and lab assets are field-map scaffolds.

Safe workflow

Unsupported content remains missing. The scripts do not open patient narratives, call external models, or make clinical or regulatory decisions.

01 · ALLOWED DATA

Synthetic, de-identified, or aggregate facts with explicit purpose, authority, and minimum necessary scope.

02 · INPUT GATE

Stop when boundaries are unmet; real PHI never enters examples, logs, or external services.

03 · PROVENANCE

Local locator, field path, value hash, and verifier role/date.

04 · ROUTING

Choose CARE, CONSORT, SPIRIT, ICH E3, or aggregate route by artifact.

05 · FAIL-CLOSED

Templates begin BLOCKED with missing/null fields; no automatic completion.

06 · VALIDATION

Structure, references, schema, arithmetic, and process documentation.

07 · HUMAN REVIEW

Qualified clinical, statistical, safety, privacy/legal, QA, regulatory, or author review.

A successful validation result does NOT replace qualified review.

Six runs. Three workflows.

Three Chinese runs and three English runs were executed and retained separately. Deterministic tools can return matching states for matching schemas; each language has its own prompt, input, commands, artifacts, and logs.
