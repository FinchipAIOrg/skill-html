03 PAIN POINT

Sensitive details are easy to miss when they are scattered through the text

Interview notes need to preserve the insight, support tickets need the problem context, and logs need the troubleshooting evidence. Names and contact details can still appear in headings, signatures, body text, or follow-up notes.

Research interviews

Preserve insights and business facts while reviewing participant names, email addresses, and phone numbers.

Support knowledge bases

Check contact details, card patterns, and signatures before a ticket becomes a public example.

Logs and collaboration

Review log excerpts for fields such as IPv4 addresses before sharing them with collaborators.

04 TRANSITION

Redaction should also tell you what changed

PII Redactor combines detection, replacement, location records, and a rule re-scan in one invocation. You receive the redacted copy, a masked findings list, and a summary, then decide whether the material is ready to share.

07 CORE ADVANTAGES

Useful as a pre-share check, with a human making the final decision

Local execution

The program uses the Python standard library, makes no network calls, and requires no API key. Pasting text into a cloud Agent may still send it to that platform.

Copy and checklist together

The output combines replacement tokens with type, line, character offsets, and masked previews for targeted review.

Selectable detector scope

All six detectors are enabled by default. A workflow can select a subset and supply extra names.

Re-scan as review evidence

The re-scan confirms that the same enabled rules did not find another match. A person still decides whether the material can be shared.

08 WHY IT MATTERS

Make redaction a standard step before material moves

Keep the source, a redacted copy, and a review record for the same material. Teams can review consistent tokens and known boundaries instead of relying on memory that the text was probably cleaned.
