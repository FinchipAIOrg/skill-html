Both sides below start from the same prompt and retrieve the same 23 articles. The only variable is whether a framework decides what those articles count as.

Two things read backwards on first contact: trap_score is reversed — 10 is safest, 1 is worst — and signals_hit only carries hits. Missed signals live in the prose state list, not in the JSON array.

Signal composition · 6 : 2

Promotion side1 · 2 · 3 · 6 · 7 · 8 — how it is being sold6
Asset side4 · 5 — fundamentals and price action2
This ratio is the capability boundary, stated as a number. Six of eight signals inspect the promotion, not the company — so what it detects is the organisation of a sales campaign, not manipulation itself. A corner no one is pushing to you will not appear.
Execution note. The two asset-side signals depend on data-fetch scripts from a companion skill that is not installed here. In this environment the honest state for them is insufficient — real coverage is 6 / 8.
04 · Workflow

Say what you heard, then let it be scanned.

Four steps. The first one is where most of the evidence is either preserved or destroyed.

01

Repeat what you heard

Paste the promoter's words verbatim. Do not tidy them into polite language.

Why — only "inside information" scores +2; "exclusive research channel" scores 0
02

Scan all eight signals

Each returns hit, miss, or insufficient, with a source. Promotional content is read as an exhibit, not as information.

Trap — no data means insufficient, never "miss"
03

Weight and classify

Keyword boost is added, hits are counted, and the total is mapped to one of four risk levels.

Read carefully — trap_score runs backwards: 10 safe → 1 worst
04

Emit report and next step

Fixed JSON plus the eight-row state list, a recommendation that cannot be blank, and forced warning phrases.

Only hits appear in signals_hit; misses live in the list
Important boundary

This answers "is this being pushed at me in an organised way?" — not "is it worth buying?" A manipulated stock that nobody is promoting to you will scan clean. A genuinely bad company with no campaign behind it will also scan clean. The detection target is the sales operation, not market manipulation in general.

Known limitations · 6

  1. Equal weighting. "Paid VIP funnel" and "templated phrasing" each count as one hit, though the first is far more serious. No weights, no confidence scores.
  2. Promotion-biased by construction. Six of eight signals inspect the sales operation. Capital-driven or accounting-fraud manipulation produces little for them to find.
  3. The data-source knot. Signal 4 judges fundamentals from published financials — and the point of a well-built fraud is to make those financials look good. The better the packaging, the weaker the signal.
  4. Result quality tracks the host model's retrieval. The same instruction run on a model that cannot search produces eight "insufficient" rows.
  5. No reference corpus. There is no blacklist or historical case library to compare against, so every run starts from scratch.
  6. Results are momentary. The scan measures whether promotion is happening now. A clean result expires; a re-run is required, not optional.
07 · Who it's for

For the moment before you start researching it.

The scan is a gate, placed upstream of analysis — not a substitute for it.

01

Retail investors

A-share holders whose information arrives through groups and short video.
Run it on any recommendation before spending an hour on the fundamentals.
02

New investors

People still calibrating what a legitimate pitch sounds like.
Paste the promoter's message unchanged — let the weighting judge the source.
03

Checking for someone else

Adult children verifying what a parent was told in a group.
The people these campaigns target and the people who run this tool are usually not the same person.
04

Advisers

Anyone who needs a first-pass risk note before a human conversation.
Use the JSON as triage material, then apply judgment on top.
